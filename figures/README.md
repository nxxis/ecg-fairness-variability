## Where the paper's numeric tables come from

The notebook does not export the paper's tables (I–XIV) to CSV — every table is printed directly
to the Colab cell output by `print_fairness_report`, `print_equalized_odds_report`,
`print_results_table`, and `run_delong_comparisons` (Blocks 9, 11, 12, 19–21). To get a table's
exact numbers, re-run the corresponding block in Colab and read them from the cell output, or
check the block-to-table map in [`../docs/reproducibility.md`](../docs/reproducibility.md).
